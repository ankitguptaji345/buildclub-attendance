What's Inside

Multi-Angle Face Recognition
Learns each member from 5 angles, so it still recognizes you from the side or a few steps away.
Real-Time Check-In/Out
Walk in, get logged automatically. Walk out, get checked out. No taps, no cards.
Leaderboard & Badges
Streaks, hour milestones, Early Bird and Night Owl badges keep members coming back.
Activity Heatmap
A GitHub-style calendar of makerspace activity over the last 26 weeks.
Admin-Gated Registration
Only an admin password can add a new face, so strangers can't register themselves.
One-Click CSV Export
Every check-in and check-out, exportable straight to Excel or Google Sheets.
